🎹 NeuralNote+ — Change History

What changed, newest first.

2026-09-01 · 3:11 PM

✨Drop Voice Memos straight into NeuralNote (M4A support)

  • NeuralNote now opens .m4a, .aac, .mp4 and .caf recordings, so Apple Voice Memos and Bloom Memo files just work.
  • Dragging a recording directly out of Voice Memos or Bloom Memo now lands in the app; before, macOS showed the "not allowed" cursor because those apps hand over a promised file rather than a path.
  • The app is renamed NeuralNote+ and installs beside the official NeuralNote without conflicts.
  • New helper scripts: scripts/install.sh builds, tests and installs; scripts/sync-upstream.sh pulls the latest official NeuralNote into this fork.
